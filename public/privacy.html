Airavat Strategies collects only the details you choose to send us, uses them only to reply to you, and never sells or shares them.

Who we are

This website, airavatstrategies.com, is run by Airavat Strategies (OPC) Private Limited ("Airavat", "we", "us"). Our office is at Alt F Coworking Space, Begumpet, Hyderabad, Telangana, India.

This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and how we handle it. It applies to this website only. Work we do for clients is covered by the confidentiality terms of each engagement.

We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it.

Consent

By sending the enquiry form you agree to us using your details for the purposes above. You can withdraw that consent at any time by writing to us, and we will stop using your details.

Who sees your information

Only the Airavat team handling your enquiry sees it. We do not sell, rent or share your details with any political party, candidate, campaign or other organisation. We do not disclose the names of people who contact us or of our clients.

We use a small number of service providers to run this website and our email, such as our web host and email provider. They store data for us and may not use it for anything else. We will share information with authorities only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

If an enquiry does not lead to an engagement, we delete your details within 12 months of our last contact. If you become a client, your information is kept under the terms of that engagement.

How we protect it

We keep enquiries in access-controlled accounts used only by the Airavat team, and we take reasonable steps to prevent loss or misuse. If a breach affecting your data occurs, we will inform you and the authorities as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you
  • correct or update it
  • delete it
  • stop using it, by withdrawing your consent

Write to us at the address below and we will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
